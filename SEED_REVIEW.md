# StayZero source review queue

**Status: candidates only. None of these links is a curated StayZero listing.** A person must open each source, check the exact claim, fee, stay rules, and current city availability, then enter the record in `src/lib/seed.ts` with their actual check date and a note saying who checked what. Reject a candidate if the source cannot support a truthful `₹0 / night` record. The app intentionally has no public curated results until that happens.

The links below were found through web research on 2026-10-11. Search snippets and automated page reads do **not** count as the product's human source check. A platform landing page supports only a platform gateway claim; it does not establish that a host will accept a traveller.
